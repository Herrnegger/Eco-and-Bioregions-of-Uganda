About this map

⚠️ This web map is a fast, informal visualization tool put together quickly to make the published classification easy to browse and check against GPS position in the field. Please refer to the paper below for more information.

Shows Uganda's hierarchical eco-zonation framework: 5 Ecoregions, subdivided into 8 Bioregion Level I, 12 Level II, and 20 Level III units (plus Lakes at each level). Derived from climate, topography, and hydrology data via unsupervised clustering, validated against potential natural vegetation. Ecological coherence is strong at the Ecoregion/BR-I level (accuracy 80.35%) and weaker at finer BR-II/BR-III levels (52.69%) — treat the finer tiers as experimental subdivisions.

Citation:
Aine, A., Graf, W., Stecher, G., Scharl, T., Ssanyu, G.A., Herrnegger, M. (2026). Hierarchical eco-zone delineation in data-scarce Afrotropical river landscapes using global datasets. International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation, 148, 105221.
