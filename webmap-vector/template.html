About this map

⚠️ This web map is a fast, informal visualization tool built to make the published classification easy to browse, click for stats, and check against GPS position in the field. Please refer to the paper below for more information.

The boundaries are drawn precisely, but the ecology isn't: the classification comes from clustering continuous environmental gradients, so real transitions between neighboring zones are gradual, not the sharp edges a polygon implies.

Click any region for its area, share of Uganda, mean elevation, and mean annual precipitation. Area is computed directly from the polygon geometry; elevation and precipitation are area-weighted zonal statistics computed from WorldClim data (rainfall reflects the 1971–2000 climate normal).

Switch Display to color regions by elevation or precipitation instead of category, using natural-breaks classification.

Citation:
Aine, A., Graf, W., Stecher, G., Scharl, T., Ssanyu, G.A., Herrnegger, M. (2026). Hierarchical eco-zone delineation in data-scarce Afrotropical river landscapes using global datasets. International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation, 148, 105221.
